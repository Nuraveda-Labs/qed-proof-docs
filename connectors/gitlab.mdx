GitLab can be connected read-only from the app today. There's no verifier for it yet, so a claim targeting GitLab
can't be decided until one ships.

<Warning>
This connector is for connecting your account now, ahead of verification. No GitLab claim can be verified yet.
</Warning>

## What it's for

Verify the pushes, merge requests and pipelines your agents claim.

## Access

- Reads the projects your account can see
- Never posts, edits or deletes

## Scopes

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `read_api` | Projects, merge requests and pipelines |
| `read_user` | Your account |
