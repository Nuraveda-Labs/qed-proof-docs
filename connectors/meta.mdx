title: Facebook Pages, Instagram, Meta Ads
description: Connect Facebook Pages, Instagram and Meta Ads read-only today. Verification for these connectors isn't built yet.
---

Facebook Pages, Instagram and Meta Ads can each be connected read-only from the app today. None of them has a
verifier yet, so a claim targeting one of these destinations can't be decided until that ships.

<Warning>
These connectors are for connecting your accounts now, ahead of verification. No `meta.*` claim can be verified yet.
</Warning>

## Facebook Pages

Verify posts your agents publish to your Pages.

- **Access:** reads posts on the Pages you pick. Never posts, edits or deletes.

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `pages_show_list` | List your Pages |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Read Page posts |
| `business_management` | Reach business-owned Pages |

## Instagram

Verify posts your agents publish to Instagram.

- **Access:** reads media on the accounts you pick. Never posts, edits or deletes.

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `instagram_basic` | Read media |
| `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` | The linked Page |
| `business_management` | Business assets |

## Meta Ads

Verify ad changes your agents make, like budget edits.

- **Access:** reads ad account activity and settings. Never changes ads or spend.

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `ads_read` | Activity log and budgets |
| `business_management` | Business-owned ad accounts |
