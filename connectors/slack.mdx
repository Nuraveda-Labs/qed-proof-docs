Slack has a live verifier today. Once your workspace is connected, QED Proof can decide a `slack.message.post` claim
by reading Slack itself — never by trusting what your agent reported.

## Access

- Reads channels you can see
- No direct messages
- Never posts, edits or deletes

## Scopes

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `channels:history`, `groups:history` | Read messages |
| `channels:read`, `groups:read` | Channel names |
| `users:read` | Who posted |
| `team:read` | Workspace name |

## What it verifies

<ParamField path="slack.message.post" type="verifier">
  Checks that a message with exactly the claimed timestamp exists in the claimed channel and, optionally, that its
  text matches a fingerprint your agent supplies. The claim's `team_id` is bound to your workspace's connection; a
  team with no connection gives `unverifiable` / `no_connection`. A `ts` present but with a different text
  fingerprint is `mismatch` / `content_mismatch`. No message with that `ts` by the deadline is `failed` /
  `not_found`. Direct messages are out of scope.

  **Target:** `slack://<team_id>/<channel_id>`. **Params:** `ts` — the message timestamp Slack returned when it was
  posted. `text_sha256` (optional) — lowercase hex SHA-256 of the normalized message text.
</ParamField>

See the <a href={`${OSS_REPO}/blob/main/spec/profiles/slack.message.post.md`}>profile</a> for the exact match rules,
