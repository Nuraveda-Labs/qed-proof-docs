Slack can be connected read-only from the app today. There's no verifier for it yet, so a claim about a Slack
message can't be decided until that ships.

<Warning>
This connector is for connecting your workspace now, ahead of verification. No `slack.*` claim can be verified yet.
</Warning>

## What it's for

Verify messages your agents post in Slack.

## Access

- Reads channels you can see
- No direct messages
- Never posts, edits or deletes

## Scopes

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `channels:history`, `groups:history` | Read messages |
| `channels:read`, `groups:read` | Channel names |
| `users:read` | Who posted |
| `team:read` | Workspace name |
