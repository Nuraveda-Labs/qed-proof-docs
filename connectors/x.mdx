X can be connected read-only from the app today. There's no verifier for it yet, so a claim about a post on X can't
be decided until that ships.

<Warning>
This connector is for connecting your account now, ahead of verification. No `x.*` claim can be verified yet.
</Warning>

## What it's for

Verify posts your agents publish on X, and that they came from your account.

## Access

- Reads posts and your account ID
- Never posts, edits or deletes

## Scopes

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `tweet.read` | Read posts |
| `users.read` | Your account |
| `offline.access` | Stay connected |
