X has a live verifier today. Once your account is connected, QED Proof can decide an `x.post.publish` claim by
reading X itself — never by trusting what your agent reported.

## Access

- Reads posts and your account ID
- Never posts, edits or deletes

## Scopes

| Scope | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `tweet.read` | Read posts |
| `users.read` | Your account |
| `offline.access` | Stay connected |

## What it verifies

<ParamField path="x.post.publish" type="verifier">
  Checks that the claimed post exists on X, was written by the account your target handle is connected as, and,
  optionally, that its text matches a fingerprint your agent supplies. The target handle is resolved to the X user
  ID of an account connected to your workspace; a handle that isn't connected gives `unverifiable` /
  `no_connection`. The post's author must equal that user ID, so a post by anyone else can't be claimed —
  `mismatch` / `target_mismatch`. If `text_sha256` is given and doesn't match, that's `mismatch` /
  `content_mismatch`. A post not found by the deadline is `failed` / `not_found`.

  **Target:** `@handle`. **Params:** `post_id` — the post's numeric ID. `text_sha256` (optional) — lowercase hex
  SHA-256 of the normalized post text.
</ParamField>

See the <a href={`${OSS_REPO}/blob/main/spec/profiles/x.post.publish.md`}>profile</a> for the exact match rules,
windows and required access.
